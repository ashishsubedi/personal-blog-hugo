## Why This Site Exists

Long story short: I like taking things apart to see how they tick. Sometimes that means writing a miniature continuous-batching LLM engine from scratch just to understand why vLLM is fast; other times it means disassembling a 193 MB Go binary at 2 AM because my weekly AI quota vanished faster than free pizza at a department seminar.

This corner of the internet holds two things:
- **[Blog](/blog/)**: Longer technical deep dives with actual measurements, packet captures, and the bugs I tripped over along the way.
- **[Dev Logs](/dlog/)**: Short field notes so Future Ashish doesn't have to re-debug what Past Ashish already solved once.
